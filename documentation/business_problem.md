# Business Problem

## Objective
Use transaction-level financial and sales data to determine what is driving revenue and profitability, where margins are being compressed, which products and customers create the most value, how regions differ, and what trends management should act on.

## Primary Stakeholder
Executive or sales/finance leadership responsible for revenue growth and profit improvement.

## Decisions This Analysis Should Support
- Which products and categories should receive more or less focus?
- Which customers or segments are most valuable?
- Which regions are over- or under-performing?
- Where are high sales failing to translate into strong profit?
- What time-based trends require management attention?

## Success Criteria
The final analysis should produce reproducible KPIs, clear supporting evidence, an executive Power BI dashboard, and a concise set of business recommendations.
